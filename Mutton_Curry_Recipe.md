# Mutton Curry Recipe

## Recipe Overview
This recipe is for a delicious mutton curry with overnight marination for best results. The base recipe serves approximately 4-6 people with 1 kg mutton.

## Instructions

### Marination (Overnight recommended)

1. **Prepare the marinade**: In a large bowl, mix yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, coriander powder, red chili powder, cumin powder, turmeric, asafoetida, and salt.

2. **Add mutton**: Add the mutton pieces to the marinade and mix well until all pieces are coated.

3. **Add aromatics**: Add slit green chilies, oil, and optional coriander leaves.

4. **Marinate**: 
   - **Best**: Refrigerate overnight (8-12 hours)
   - **Minimum**: 1 hour at room temperature

### Cooking Process

1. **Prepare spices**: Heat ghee and oil in a heavy-bottomed pot. Add crushed black cardamom, cloves, black peppercorns, bay leaves, cinnamon, and a pinch of stone flower.

2. **Fry spices**: Fry the whole spices for 1 minute until fragrant.

3. **Cook onions**: Add small onions and fry for a few minutes until they start to soften.

4. **Season and add tomatoes**: Add salt, then add finely diced tomatoes. Fry for several minutes.

5. **Add ground spices**: Add turmeric, red chili powder, and coriander powder. Mix well.

6. **Cook base**: Continue cooking until onions turn golden brown and tomatoes are well cooked (this takes time - don't rush or burn).

7. **Add mutton**: Add the marinated mutton along with all the marinade. Fry until you see color change in the meat.

8. **Slow cook**: Add water as needed, add meat masala, and cook covered until mutton is tender (45 minutes to 1.5 hours depending on cut).

9. **Final touches**: Once mutton is cooked, add 1-2 pinches more meat masala. Reduce excess liquid if needed.

10. **Garnish**: Add a generous amount of fresh coriander leaves with stems. Mix and serve.

## Cooking Tips

- **Marination time**: Overnight marination gives the best flavor
- **Heat control**: Cook tomatoes thoroughly but avoid burning
- **Water quantity**: Add just enough water to cook the mutton - too much will make it watery
- **Doneness test**: Mutton should be fork-tender when done
- **Resting**: Let the curry rest for 10-15 minutes after cooking for flavors to meld

## Serving Suggestions

- Serve hot with basmati rice, naan, or roti
- Garnish with additional fresh coriander and sliced onions
- Best enjoyed with a dollop of yogurt on the side

## Storage

- **Refrigerator**: 3-4 days
- **Freezer**: Up to 3 months
- **Reheating**: Add a splash of water if needed when reheating
