# 🍛 Authentic Mutton Curry

*Rich, aromatic, and tender mutton curry with overnight marination*

---

## 📋 Recipe Info
- **Serves:** 4-6 people
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes + overnight marination
- **Cook Time:** 1.5-2 hours
- **Total Time:** ~12 hours (including marination)

---

## 🥘 Ingredients

### For the Marinade
- **1 kg** mutton, cut into pieces
- **1½ cups** yogurt (dahi)
- **2 tbsp** ginger-garlic paste
- **2½ tbsp** coriander powder
- **1½ tbsp** red chili powder (Kashmiri preferred)
- **1 tsp** cumin powder
- **2 tsp** turmeric (haldi)
- **½ tsp** asafoetida (hing)
- **3** green chilies, slit vertically
- **1 tbsp** oil
- **Salt** to taste
- **Fresh coriander leaves** (optional)

### For the Curry
- **2-2½ tbsp** ghee
- **Oil** as needed
- **2** black cardamom, crushed
- **3-4** cloves
- **8** black peppercorns
- **2** bay leaves, broken in half
- **1 inch** cinnamon stick, broken
- **Small pinch** stone flower (dagad phool)
- **6-8** small onions
- **2-3** tomatoes, finely diced
- **1 tsp** turmeric
- **2 tsp** red chili powder
- **3 tsp** coriander powder
- **1½ tbsp** meat masala
- **Large bunch** fresh coriander with stems

---

## 👨‍🍳 Instructions

### 🌙 Marination (Overnight Recommended)

1. **Prepare the marinade** - In a large bowl, mix yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, coriander powder, red chili powder, cumin powder, turmeric, asafoetida, and salt.

2. **Coat the mutton** - Add mutton pieces to the marinade and mix well until all pieces are thoroughly coated.

3. **Add aromatics** - Add slit green chilies, oil, and optional coriander leaves.

4. **Marinate**
   - **🏆 Best:** Refrigerate overnight (8-12 hours)
   - **⚡ Quick:** Minimum 1 hour at room temperature

### 🔥 Cooking Process

1. **Heat the base** - In a heavy-bottomed pot, heat ghee and oil. Add crushed black cardamom, cloves, black peppercorns, bay leaves, cinnamon, and stone flower.

2. **Bloom the spices** - Fry whole spices for 1 minute until fragrant.

3. **Cook onions** - Add small onions and fry for a few minutes until they start to soften.

4. **Add tomatoes** - Season with salt, then add finely diced tomatoes. Fry for several minutes.

5. **Ground spices** - Add turmeric, red chili powder, and coriander powder. Mix well.

6. **Build the base** - Continue cooking until onions turn golden brown and tomatoes are well cooked. *Take your time - don't rush or burn!*

7. **Add marinated mutton** - Add the mutton along with all the marinade. Fry until you see color change in the meat.

8. **Slow cook to perfection** - Add water as needed, add meat masala, cover and cook until mutton is fork-tender (45 minutes to 1.5 hours).

9. **Final seasoning** - Once mutton is cooked, add 1-2 pinches more meat masala. Reduce excess liquid if needed.

10. **Garnish and serve** - Add a generous amount of fresh coriander leaves with stems. Mix and serve hot!

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **⏰ Marination:** Overnight marination gives the best flavor
- **🔥 Heat control:** Cook tomatoes thoroughly but avoid burning  
- **💧 Water wisdom:** Add just enough water - too much makes it watery
- **🍴 Doneness test:** Mutton should be fork-tender when ready
- **😌 Rest time:** Let curry rest 10-15 minutes after cooking

---

## 🍽️ Serving & Storage

**Serve with:** Basmati rice, naan, or roti  
**Garnish:** Fresh coriander and sliced onions  
**Perfect pairing:** A dollop of yogurt on the side

**Storage:**
- 🧊 Refrigerator: 3-4 days
- ❄️ Freezer: Up to 3 months  
- 🔥 Reheating: Add splash of water if needed
